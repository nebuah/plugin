# Regulatory Compliance Review

## Steps
1. Identify all applicable regulatory bodies and their jurisdictional authority
2. Map the regulatory framework — statutes, regulations, guidance documents, enforcement actions
3. Inventory the client's current compliance measures and documentation
4. Perform gap analysis — compare current state against regulatory requirements
5. Assess risk severity for each identified gap (high/medium/low)
6. Prioritize remediation: address high-risk items first, then medium, then low
7. Draft compliance recommendations with specific actionable steps
8. Document findings in a compliance report with citations to specific regulatory provisions

## Negative Constraints
- Never assume a regulation has not changed — always verify the current version and effective date
- Never provide compliance advice without checking for recent enforcement actions or guidance updates
- Never treat compliance as a one-time exercise — it requires ongoing monitoring
- Never downplay regulatory risk without documenting the basis for the risk assessment

## Notes
- Regulatory landscapes change frequently — flag areas where monitoring is needed
- Consider both the letter and spirit of regulations; enforcement trends matter
- Multi-jurisdictional operations require compliance mapping for each jurisdiction
