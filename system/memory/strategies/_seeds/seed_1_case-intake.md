# New Case/Engagement Intake Pattern

## Steps
1. Gather and document client information, key contacts, and matter details
2. Define the scope of engagement — objectives, deliverables, timeline, and budget
3. Identify applicable jurisdictions and governing law
4. Conduct preliminary conflict of interest check
5. Create case/project directory structure with standard folders (research, drafts, correspondence, filings)
6. Identify key legal issues and flag areas requiring immediate attention
7. Compile initial document index of materials received from client
8. Draft engagement letter or scope memo for client confirmation

## Negative Constraints
- Do not begin substantive legal work before confirming engagement scope with client
- Do not assume jurisdiction without verifying — always confirm applicable law
- Do not skip the conflict check — it is an ethical obligation
- Do not store client-privileged materials outside secure project directories

## Notes
- Use the case directory structure consistently across all engagements for Dream Engine pattern recognition
- Always check for related prior engagements that may contain reusable strategies
